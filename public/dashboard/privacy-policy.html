OmniBot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 21 August 2026

OmniBot stores server configuration, moderation records, economy data, and appeal submissions as needed to provide features.

Discord OAuth provides your user id, username, and guild list for dashboard login. Secrets and bot tokens are never exposed to the browser.

Third parties (Discord, Groq, hosting providers, image APIs) process data under their own policies when features are used.
